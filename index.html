Deal Analyst Program:
Hands-on Dealmaking.

Scroll to explore

Analyze. Model. Pitch. In Teams.

How the 1–2 Week Sprint Projects work.

1

New Batches Every 1–2 Weeks

New projects are released across multiple topics. You sign up for what interests you most and can pause during exam periods. Complete flexibility to learn at your own pace.

2

Small Teams, Guided by Mentors

Work in agile cohorts of 4–5 analysts with direct guidance from a mentor who will help in every step. Work with teammates and learn together.

3

Deliverable & Next Rotation

Submit a report, investment memo or financial model with your team, receive feedback, and pick your next project when the upcoming batch launches.

Project topics & coverage.

Transaction & Deal Analysis

End-to-end analysis of landmark buyouts, growth equity rounds, and venture investments.

PE Firms & Fund Economics

How top GPs operate: investment theses, management fees, carried interest, and portfolio governance.

Valuation & LBO Modeling

Financial modeling in Excel from scratch: DCF valuation, trading comps, debt waterfalls, and returns sensitivity.

Interview & Technical Preparation

Paper LBOs, financial modeling tests, and case-study drills designed to clear recruitment rounds in high finance.

Thematic Focus Groups

Specialized research tracks in emerging verticals: Asset Tokenization, Private Credit, Energy, and Infrastructure.

Application Process

How to apply as an Analyst.

No prior finance background is required. We evaluate intellectual curiosity, motivation, and the willingness to learn in team environments.

01

Select a Deal

Pick any real PE or VC transaction (historical or recent) that genuinely sparks your interest.

02

Analyze the Core

Research the macro context, key players, transaction rationale, and strategic value creation. It is essential that you thoroughly understand the business and deal mechanics.

03

Complete the Report

Follow the provided template below. No need to maintain aesthetics, just follow the structure. Include a short motivation note: Why do you want to join the club? (Don't use AI for this one!)

04

Submit Application

Upload your report via the form during active intake windows. Applications for Q1 2026/27 are now closed. if you have any questions.
